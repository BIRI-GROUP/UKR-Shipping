- China → Syria: LCL/FCL/Air/multimodal/DDP where confirmed
- UAE → Syria: Air, Air DDP, Road FTL
- Syria → UAE: Road FTL via supported corridor
- UAE ↔ Turkey: Road FTL
- UAE → KSA/Qatar/Bahrain/Kuwait/Jordan/Syria: Road FTL / supported equipment
- China → Worldwide: LCL/FCL/Air/door/DDP where confirmed
- Worldwide → UAE: LCL/FCL/Air based on capability
- UAE → Worldwide: LCL/FCL/Air export and door where confirmed
- China domestic: courier, pickup, trucking, warehouse/port/airport delivery
- UAE domestic: Jebel Ali/DXB/DWC/city pickup and delivery
