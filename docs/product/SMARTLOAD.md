# UKR SmartLoad

SmartLoad optimizes compatible capacity across LCL, Air and Road.

Customer products:
- SMART
- PRIORITY
- EXPRESS

Internal optimization can consider CBM, payload, chargeable weight, dates, route, commodity, DG, stackability, service promise, capacity and contribution.

Customer-facing pricing never exposes UKR buy rates, counterparties or optimization margin.
Initial implementation is rules-based with staff approval.
