Delivery with clear expectations

Cargo handover is at the building entrance or agreed ground-level point. Meet our team downstairs; apartment and upstairs delivery are not included. Free collection is available in Ras Al Khor, Dubai.

Allow a 4-hour delivery window. Standard waiting is 5 minutes, up to 15 minutes maximum. Sea-cargo local delivery typically takes 1–3 days; air cargo 8–48 hours after release and readiness are confirmed.

Missed delivery may incur a redelivery fee. Any applicable 50% charge and its basis will be confirmed first. No further attempt follows the second attempt. Free Dubai delivery excludes free zones, restricted areas and locations outside city limits.
